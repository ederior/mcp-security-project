Three research questions, one per click, each tied to a metric: attack success rate; the drop in that rate from detection and from mitigation, plus false alarms and added latency; and the hardening recommendations in the final report. On the right, the assumptions and constraints: everything runs against our own local server; success is measured with harmless fake secrets ("canaries") and fake users; network-layer attacks such as TLS, and attacks on the model's weights, are out of scope.
